# v2.1.0 original asset prompts

All three assets were generated as original transparent PNG cutouts, visually reviewed, then exported as WebP at 720 px maximum dimension for Android. Their source PNGs are in `art-source/`; optimized files are in `app/src/main/assets/art/`.

Shared direction: polished friendly 2.5D isometric mobile farming illustration, camera about 30 degrees above, warm daylight from upper left, soft shadow below right, textured but readable at small size, consistent proportions, centered isolated object, transparent background, no text, logo, watermark, emojis, or copyrighted game references.

1. **Village fruit orchard:** A small Cambodian village orchard with mango and banana trees, a modest raised wooden shelter with a warm terracotta roof, woven produce baskets, tropical greenery, and a clear silhouette. Match the established family home art's angle, lighting, and soft painted texture. Source: `art-source/fruit-orchard.png`; runtime: `art/fruit-orchard.webp`.
2. **Village community hall:** A modest open-sided Cambodian countryside wooden meeting pavilion, slightly raised with steps, a warm terracotta roof, woven mats and clay vessels, surrounded by sparse greenery. No religious symbols. Match the same game camera and light. Source: `art-source/community-hall.png`; runtime: `art/community-hall.webp`.
3. **River produce boat:** A small Cambodian produce boat with a woven canopy, baskets and produce, and a modest motor, seen in the same 2.5D angle with warm light and soft shadow. Source: `art-source/river-boat.png`; runtime: `art/river-boat.webp`.

Specific architecture and regional details should be checked with Cambodian art reviewers before a larger public campaign. These images are game illustrations, not documentary depictions of a particular real village.
